## Dating-app safety

- **Gradual disclosure:** share surname, workplace, address, and other identifiers late, if at all.
- **Boundaries:** decide in advance what you will not share (e.g., intimate images) and don't renegotiate under pressure.
- **Stay in-app** until trust is established; be wary of pushes to move to another platform quickly.
- **Verify:** use in-app verification, video call before meeting, reverse-image search profile photos.
- **Meet safely:** public place, tell someone you trust, own transport.
- **Block and report** at the first pressure, money request, or threat.
- Red flags: requests for money, rushed intimacy, refusal to video call, inconsistent story.

If pressured or threatened: [sextortion](../incident-response/sextortion.md) or [harassment](../incident-response/harassment-and-impersonation.md).

## Preserving evidence safely if threatened

1. Don't delete the conversation. Take screenshots showing usernames, dates, and the threat.
2. Save profile URLs/IDs in an **off-repo**, access-protected place (not this repo).
3. Don't store or forward intimate content, including as "evidence"; describe it instead. Don't send it to anyone.
4. Report in-app, then to police if there's a credible threat.
5. Log facts using [the redacted template](../templates/incident-log-redacted.md).
