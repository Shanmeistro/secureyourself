## Phishing habits

- Don't act from the link; navigate to the service directly or use the password manager.
- Urgency, threats, gift offers, and unexpected attachments are signals, not proof.
- Verify unusual requests through a second channel.
- If you clicked or entered anything: [phishing playbook](../incident-response/phishing-and-social-engineering.md).
