# Harassment and Impersonation

[← Incident response index](README.md)

## 1. Trigger
- Repeated unwanted contact, threats, doxxing
- A fake profile or account using my name/photos

## 2. Immediate priorities
1. **Personal safety first.** If there is a credible immediate threat, contact emergency services (`[LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER]`).
2. Secure my accounts.
3. Preserve evidence, then report.

## 3. First 15 minutes
1. Stop replying. Don't escalate or confront.
2. Screenshot messages/profile with dates and usernames.
3. Block/mute and report through the platform.
4. From a trusted device: change passwords, review sessions and recovery methods ([account compromise](account-compromise.md)).

## 4. First 24 hours
1. Tighten privacy settings ([social](../communications/social-and-dating-apps.md)).
2. Report impersonation using the platform's impersonation form.
3. Tell a trusted person; agree check-ins.
4. If you know the person or physical safety is at risk, consider contacting police or a local support service (`[LOCAL SUPPORT SERVICE]`).
5. Warn close contacts about the fake account.

## 5. Recovery and verification
- Fake content reported; track report status
- Accounts clean; recovery methods mine
- Run a [footprint review](../privacy/public-footprint.md)
- Note: platforms and authorities may not remove all content; plan for ongoing monitoring

## 6. Evidence to preserve
- Dated screenshots with URLs/handles, saved off-repo with restricted access
- Report reference numbers
- Log summary in the [redacted log](../templates/incident-log-redacted.md)

## 7. Who to contact
- The platform's reporting and safety teams
- Local police for threats, stalking, or doxxing
- Employer/HR if work is involved
- Local victim-support or legal advice services (`[LOCAL SUPPORT SERVICE]`)

## 8. What not to do
- Don't engage, argue, or retaliate
- Don't meet the person to resolve it
- Don't delete evidence
- Don't assume the platform or police will remove everything

## 9. After-action review
What information was exposed and how? Update privacy settings, the [threat model](../threat-model/personal-threat-model.md), and support contacts.
