# Sextortion

[← Incident response index](README.md)

## 1. Trigger
Someone threatens to share intimate images or information unless I pay or send more.

## 2. Immediate priorities
1. **Personal safety and wellbeing first.** You are not to blame; this is a crime run by organised scammers or individuals.
2. Stop the contact.
3. Don't pay and don't send more material.
4. If there is a credible immediate threat, call emergency services (`[LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER]`).

## 3. First 15 minutes
1. Stop replying; don't negotiate.
2. Don't pay; payment usually leads to more demands.
3. Screenshot the threats, profile, and payment demands (not the intimate content itself where avoidable).
4. Report and block through the platform.
5. From a trusted device, secure accounts and tighten privacy; hide friend lists.

## 4. First 24 hours
1. Report to police or a national reporting service (`[LOCAL REPORTING SERVICE]`).
2. Use platform tools to report intimate-image abuse; some services help to hash/block known images (`Needs verification`).
3. Tell a trusted person for support.
4. If the person is under 18, contact specialist child-protection services immediately.
5. Deactivate (don't delete) accounts if that helps, after preserving evidence.

## 5. Recovery and verification
- Accounts secured; sessions reviewed
- Monitor for posting; report quickly
- Note that no platform or authority can promise removal of all content
- Seek emotional support; counselling is reasonable

## 6. Evidence to preserve
- Usernames, profile links, message text, payment details requested
- Dates/times
- Store off-repo with restricted access; never in this repo; don't forward intimate content

## 7. Who to contact
- Local police / cybercrime reporting
- The platform's safety team
- Victim-support helplines (`[LOCAL SUPPORT SERVICE]`)
- Trusted friend/family member

## 8. What not to do
- Don't pay or send more images
- Don't threaten or confront the person
- Don't delete the chat before saving evidence
- Don't continue the conversation 'to see what they'll do'
- Don't blame yourself

## 9. After-action review
What was the contact path? Update [dating-app habits](../communications/social-and-dating-apps.md) and the [threat model](../threat-model/personal-threat-model.md).
