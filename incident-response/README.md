# Incident Response

Use these when something is happening. **Breathe. Safety first, then accounts, then evidence.** Do the steps from a device you trust (not the compromised one).

| Situation | Playbook |
|---|---|
| Account taken over or suspicious sign-in | [account-compromise.md](account-compromise.md) |
| Clicked a link / shared info with a scammer | [phishing-and-social-engineering.md](phishing-and-social-engineering.md) |
| Phone/laptop lost or stolen | [lost-or-stolen-device.md](lost-or-stolen-device.md) |
| Harassment or fake profile | [harassment-and-impersonation.md](harassment-and-impersonation.md) |
| Threat to share intimate images | [sextortion.md](sextortion.md) |

**Emergency:** if anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (`[LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER]`) first.

Each playbook has the same nine headings: Trigger, Immediate priorities, First 15 minutes, First 24 hours, Recovery and verification, Evidence to preserve, Who to contact, What not to do, After-action review.
