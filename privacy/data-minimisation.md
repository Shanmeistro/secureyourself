# Data Minimisation

Last reviewed: `[YYYY-MM-DD]`. Data I don't hold or share can't be leaked, so fewer assets means less to protect.

## Principles

1. Share the minimum a service needs; use aliases/masked email where sensible (and know how recovery works with them).
2. Delete accounts and data I no longer use.
3. Keep minimal local data on phones, especially when travelling ([travel kit](travel-privacy-kit.md)).
4. Grant the least app permissions; revisit quarterly.
5. Keep sensitive documents out of email and chat; store in an encrypted location.
6. Nothing sensitive in this repo ([SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)).

## Quarterly checklist

- [ ] Delete unused accounts (start from the password-manager list)
- [ ] Remove old files from cloud storage and downloads
- [ ] Review app permissions and contact syncing
- [ ] Check retention settings on messaging and email
- [ ] Review what each SSO provider shares ([SSO](../identity-and-accounts/sso-decisions.md))
- [ ] Unsubscribe from data-collecting marketing

See also [public footprint](public-footprint.md).
